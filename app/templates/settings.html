Build guidance presets

Each preset becomes a Use <name> guidance button in a project's Coding settings. Choose files… browses this machine's folders: tick .md files or folders and add them; a folder adds the .md files it holds at that moment. The preset keeps that fixed list and the Coding button copies it, so pick again after a skill gains files. Up to 32 presets; a preset and a project each take at most 32 files and 256 KB, added to every build turn.

Approval steps in guidance (for example brainstorming's design approval or writing-plans' plan approval) do not stop a build run: the other agents approve instead, and you are asked only if they have not agreed within 3 cycles.

Leave out finishing-work and using-git-worktrees: they merge, push or work outside the project, and Delibra makes the build commits itself.
